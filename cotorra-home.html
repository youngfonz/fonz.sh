What a clip does that a flashcard can’t.

Word lists hand you vocabulary out of context. Cotorra gives you the sentence a person would actually say, then lets you take it apart.

TopicA flashcard appCotorra

Four moves, then the next clip.

  1. Watch

    Scroll to a clip and hear the line. Tap Slow when it goes by too fast.

  2. Tap

    Any word you don’t know opens its meaning and how to say it.

  3. Say it

    Read the line aloud. Each word turns green when you land it.

  4. Review

    Words you saved come back on a schedule, just before you’d forget them.
