Aero

Live flight tracker + browser flight sim · personal build

The tracker

A live arrivals board for San José, Van Nuys and Teterboro. Every plane in the air on one map, a landing log with flight, origin, airline, aircraft and tail number, and any tail number looked up on demand.

The flight sim

A flight school that runs in the browser, set at SJC. Pick a Cessna 172, an Embraer 175 or a Gulfstream G550, then take a 19-step guided lesson, practice landings or fly free. An instructor and the tower talk you through it out loud.

266Landings logged at SJC on Monday, Nov 2. The log has run every day since Sep 28.

Cadence

Speaking tutor · Spanish · Mandarin · Arabic

What it is

A private speaking tutor for three languages: Spanish, Mandarin and Arabic, each with its own tutor. You speak out loud every day; the tutor corrects you against this week's grammar and vocabulary, and answers in a real voice.

The plan

Spanish runs on a 52-week curriculum from A1 to B2, in four phases. Each week has a grammar focus, a vocabulary domain, three scenes and a listening assignment. Your mic audio never leaves the device; only the transcript does.

612 hrsWhat B2 costs an English speaker. 156 of them are speaking in the app.
